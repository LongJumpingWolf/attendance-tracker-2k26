Homepage concepts

Ten standalone versions of the Today screen. Same data, ten different ideas. Open each on your phone, or in a browser at phone width.

ROUND 3 · AFTER EVERYTHING IS MARKED

CONCEPT 12All done

Five ideas for the Today tab once every class is resolved: a day ring, tomorrow first, an attendance pass with stamps, a skip budget, and a choose-your-own widget (skip forecast, where you stand, or your next deadline) with a first-time empty state. Light and dark.

ROUND 2 · INTERACTIVE METAPHORS

CONCEPT 6Skip jar

Your skip budget as real marbles in a jar, with physics. Marking absent pulls one out, attending earns marbles back. Shake it with your finger.

 CONCEPT 7Departures board

A split-flap airport board. Every class is a flight, and marking it flips the letters to PRESENT or ABSENT.

 CONCEPT 8Safe knob

Turn a rotary knob clockwise to check in, counter-clockwise to mark absent. Ticks light up as you turn, with sound and haptics.

 CONCEPT 9Orbit

Subjects are planets circling the clock. The closer to “now”, the sooner the class. Drag one onto the Present or Absent dock.

 CONCEPT 10Stamp pad

Each class is a passport page. Drag a rubber stamp onto it, feel the thunk, and the page flies away.

ROUND 1 · LAYOUTS

CONCEPT 1Timeline

Your day as a calendar with a live “now” line. Tap a class block, mark it in the dock below.

 CONCEPT 2Swipe deck

One big card at a time. Swipe right for present, left for absent.

 CONCEPT 3Dial

A watch-face. Every class is an arc on the dial, a hand shows the time.

 CONCEPT 4Bento

Colourful tiles. Each subject tile flips over to mark it.

 CONCEPT 5Conversation

A chat that asks about each class, and answers “can I skip physiology?”.

Each file is self-contained (one HTML file, no build step). Marks are kept in memory only, so refresh to reset.
